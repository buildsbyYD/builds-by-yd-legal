Privacy Policy

Revonate – Home & Vehicle Manager
Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Revonate. This Privacy Policy explains how Revonate (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) manages information when you use our Android application. We respect your privacy and design our applications around local data retention.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Revonate operates without requiring user account registration, login credentials, or cloud profile creation.

  • No Central Server Storage: We do not operate external application servers that store your personal records.
  • User Data: All information entered into the App—including home details and purchase prices, improvement and repair expenses, vehicle specs (make, model, VIN, license plates, mileage), maintenance records, photos and receipts you attach, reports, to-dos, and reminders—is stored exclusively on your device.

3. Local Storage, Backups, & File Management

  • Device Storage: Your records are kept in a local database, and photos you attach are copied into the App's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read them.
  • Backups You Create: The Export feature creates a backup file (.zip) containing your records and attached photos, saved to a location you choose. The optional Auto backup feature (Premium) saves the same file automatically to a folder you choose. If you pick a folder in a cloud storage app (such as Google Drive), that file is stored by that provider under your own account. We have no access to any backup file.
  • Android System Backup: If Android's built-in backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the App's data in your device backup to your Google account. This backup is managed by Google, and we have no access to it.
  • CSV and PDF Exports: Spreadsheet (CSV) and PDF reports are generated on your device and saved to a location you choose. We never transmit these files off your device.

4. In-App Purchases & Billing

Revonate offers an optional one-time Premium upgrade through Google Play Billing.

  • Payment Processing: Transactions are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see, process, or store your credit card details or billing address.
  • Purchase Tokens: Google Play sends a secure digital token to the App to confirm that a premium license exists. This verification happens locally or via Google Play Services on your device.

5. Permissions & Device Features Used by the App

To deliver its features locally, Revonate uses the following:

  • Notifications: To send you reminders for home tasks and vehicle maintenance.
  • Alarms & Reminders: To deliver reminders at the exact time you set.
  • Run at Startup: To restore your scheduled reminders after your device restarts.
  • Photos & Camera: When you choose to add a photo, the App uses Android's photo picker or your camera app. The App only receives the photos you select or take; it cannot browse your photo library and does not request camera or storage permissions.
  • Files: When you export, import, or choose a backup folder, the App uses Android's system file picker and can only access the files and folders you choose.

6. Third-Party Services

Revonate does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, cloud user-tracking tools, or behavioral analytics frameworks. The only external system interaction occurs with Google Play Services for license verification and in-app billing.

7. Biometric & Device Security

If your device utilizes screen locks, passcodes, or biometrics (fingerprint/facial recognition), your local Revonate data benefits from your device’s native hardware-level encryption. We do not collect or access biometric data.

8. Data Retention & Permanent Deletion

Because all data resides on your physical device, you retain complete control over data removal. You can permanently erase all records and attached photos at any time by:

  • Clearing the application data in your Android system settings (Settings > Apps > Revonate > Storage > Clear Data), or
  • Uninstalling the Revonate app from your device.

Backup, CSV, and PDF files you have saved elsewhere are not removed this way; you can delete them yourself.

9. Children’s Privacy

Revonate does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13, as the App does not collect personal data from any user.

10. Rights under CCPA & GDPR

Because Revonate does not collect, transmit, sell, or process personal data on external servers, there is no remote personal data for us to access, rectify, port, or erase. All management of your personal information remains strictly within your control on your Android device.

11. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia, United States.
