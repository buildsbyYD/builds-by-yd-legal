Privacy Policy

TripKit – Travel Assistant

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TripKit. This Privacy Policy explains how TripKit (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use our Android travel assistant application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being fully transparent about our data practices.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. TripKit has no account sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking, and we do not run any server that receives your data.

Everything you enter into the App — including trips, dates, destinations, packing and souvenir lists, budgets, and Vault contents — is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

3. Local Data Storage & Secure Vault

All App data is stored on your device in encrypted storage (AES-256). This includes the Secure Vault, which can hold:

  • Flight details and booking references
  • Places you are staying (names, addresses, landmarks, phone numbers)
  • Emergency information you choose to enter, such as allergies, blood type, medications, an emergency contact, and travel insurance details
  • Document photos (for example, a passport or visa) and personal notes

Vault photos are saved as encrypted files in the App’s private storage. The Vault is protected by your PIN and, if you choose, your device’s biometric unlock. While the Vault is open, the App blocks screenshots and screen recording of it.

We cannot access this data. Because we have no server, we cannot retrieve, read, or restore your Vault data if you forget your PIN or lose your device.

Please note: depending on your device settings, Android may automatically back up App data to your personal Google account. We do not have access to these backups, and you can manage or turn off this feature in your device’s Android settings.

4. Backup Files You Create

TripKit lets you save a backup file of your trips and settings to a location you choose (for example, your device storage or a cloud drive you use). We never receive these files.

  • Vault text (such as booking references, addresses, emergency information, and notes) is included only if you enter your Vault PIN when creating the backup.
  • Vault photos are never included in a backup.
  • Backup files are not encrypted. Anyone who can open the file can read its contents, so store it somewhere private.

5. Internet Access & Third-Party Services

TripKit works offline except for one feature: fetching currency exchange rates. To get rates, the App requests public exchange-rate data from open.er-api.com (ExchangeRate-API), with backup sources served through cdn.jsdelivr.net and pages.dev. These requests contain only currency codes (for example, “USD” and “EUR”).

As with any internet request, these providers can see your device’s IP address and basic technical details of the request, under their own privacy policies. No personal data, trip details, or Vault information is ever sent. The latest rates are saved on your device so conversions keep working offline.

6. App Permissions

  • Internet: Used only to fetch exchange rates, as described above.
  • Notifications: Used on Android 13 or higher if you turn on packing reminders. Reminders are scheduled and shown entirely on your device and are not sent through any external messaging server.
  • Biometrics: Used only if you choose to unlock your Vault with fingerprint or face recognition.
  • Photos: TripKit uses Android’s photo picker, so it sees only the specific photos you choose to add to your Vault. It does not need access to your full photo library.

Buttons such as “Call” open your phone’s dialer with the number filled in. The App does not place calls or read your call history or contacts.

7. Biometric Authentication

TripKit offers an optional biometric lock for the Secure Vault, using your device’s built-in biometric system. The App never accesses, collects, or stores your biometric data. All authentication is handled entirely by the Android operating system.

8. Children’s Privacy

TripKit does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

9. Data Security

Because all data stays on your device, its security also depends on your device’s own protections, such as a screen lock, device encryption, and up-to-date software. We recommend keeping these enabled.

10. Data Deletion

You can delete App data at any time by:

  • Deleting a trip, or using Settings > Erase everything to remove all trips, lists, and the Vault.
  • Using Forgot PIN? on the Vault lock screen, which erases all Vault data.
  • Uninstalling TripKit, or going to Settings > Apps > TripKit > Clear Data on your Android device.

Warning: Uninstalling the App or clearing its data permanently deletes all photos stored in the Secure Vault. Backup files you saved yourself are not deleted by the App; you can delete them wherever you stored them.

11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA) & European Users (GDPR)

Because TripKit does not collect, sell, or share any personal data, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct, export, delete, or opt out of. All data you enter stays on your device under your control.
